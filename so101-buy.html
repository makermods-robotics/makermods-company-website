[ WHAT YOU ARE CHOOSING ]

The SO-101 options are organized by arm count: a standard pair has 1 leader + 1 follower; bimanual has 2 leaders + 2 followers. The bimanual OpenBooth bundle adds the booth frame and 2 cameras.

[ COMPUTER + TRAINING ]

Bring a computer for setup, teleoperation, recording, and policy work. A GPU is not required to drive or record the arms; training requirements depend on the policy you select.

[ MAKERMODS LAB ]

Use MakerMods Lab to connect and calibrate the arms, teleoperate the follower, record datasets, train policies, and evaluate results. Hugging Face publishes the official open-source SO-101 setup documentation.
