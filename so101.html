Built to teach by showing.

SO-101 is a paired teleoperation system. Guide the low-resistance leader arm through a task; the follower reproduces the motion while MakerMods Lab captures the camera views, observations, and actions that become training data.

[ FAQ · 07 ]

Before your first episode.

You guide the leader arm by hand and the powered follower mirrors it. MakerMods Lab records the follower's motion and connected camera views as demonstrations for robot-learning datasets.
Use MakerMods Lab to connect and calibrate the arms, teleoperate the follower, record tasks such as pick-and-place, train a policy, and evaluate the learned behavior.
Not for MakerMods Lab calibration, teleoperation, or recording. Training time and hardware depend on the policy and compute target you choose.
OpenBooth gives the arms repeatable camera views and a consistent visual workspace. It also connects the setup to a public catalog of SO-101 recordings on Hugging Face, so experiments can start from shared data.
Yes. Hugging Face publishes the official SO-101 setup documentation, and The Robot Studio's SO-ARM100 repository contains the bill of materials, printable files, optional hardware, and simulation assets under the Apache-2.0 license.
